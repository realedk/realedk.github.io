Layout Manager

Create and edit frac missile and HP line layouts. Add spools, blocks, valves, ports, and stands. Set lengths, move and join components, and export the layout as an image.

NOTE: macOS App is currently not signed! After opening it, you will get a popup telling you to either move it to the trash can, or close the app. Go to Settings > Privacy and Security > Open Anyway. Open the app again and click "Open Anyway". Signed version coming soon. App Store version planned.
